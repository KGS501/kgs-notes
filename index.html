KGS Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

KGS Notes is a note-taking app for notes kept on your device or on a Nextcloud server you choose. It has no ads, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated backend service, and it does not create a KGS Notes account.

Data Stored On Your Device

Notes in the Local Source are stored as Markdown files in the app's private storage, together with the images you add to them, notes in Trash, categories, and your settings. Notes from a Nextcloud Source are kept on the device as working copies so they stay available without a connection.

Nextcloud Sources

If you add a Nextcloud Source, you sign in through your server in your browser, or with an app password. The app stores the server address, login name, and app password on your device, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. Notes, server addresses, login names, and app passwords are sent only to the server you configure, and only over HTTPS, so syncing can work.

Photos

When you add a photo to a note, you choose it through the Android photo picker. The app receives only the photos you pick and does not request access to your photo library or storage. A copy of each photo is stored with the note. Photos can contain metadata such as location and camera details; under Settings, Photos & privacy, you can have the app ask each time, remove this metadata, or keep the original photo.

Editor And Links

The note editor runs inside the app from files bundled with it. Images linked in a note may be loaded from the address in the note; that server receives the image request. Remote resources are blocked when previewing a file opened from another app. Links such as Sponsor this project, Report a bug, Source code, and License open in your browser, where the linked website's own privacy practices apply.

Files Opened From Other Apps

You can open Markdown and text files through another app. These files stay separate from your KGS Notes Sources unless you choose to save a copy there. If the other app grants write access, edits can be saved back to the original file. Temporary recovery copies are kept in private storage excluded from Android backup.

Backups

Android app backup and device-to-device transfer may include Local Source content (notes, their images, and Trash) according to your device and Android backup settings. Nextcloud credentials, working copies of Nextcloud notes, and synchronization data are excluded. On Android 8.0 and 8.1, Android does not guarantee that cloud backups are encrypted on the device before upload.

Logs

The app does not write note content, credentials, or server addresses to its logs.

Deleting Local Data

Notes deleted permanently from Trash are removed from the device. Removing a Nextcloud Source in Settings deletes its credentials and working copies from the device; notes on the server stay unchanged. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its local data from the device.
